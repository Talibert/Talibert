### 👨‍💻 About Me

- 💼 **Software Developer** at **Élin Duxus Consultoria**, building resilient backend solutions and enterprise applications.
- 🎓 Graduated in **Industrial Automation** (FATEC) and currently pursuing a Bachelor's Degree in **Information Systems**.
- ⚙️ Focused on **Distributed Architectures**, **Event-Driven Systems** (**Kafka**, **RabbitMQ**), and **Cloud & Containerization** (**Docker**, **Kubernetes**).
- 🗄️ Experienced in modeling and querying high-performance databases with **Oracle**, **SQL Server**, **PostgreSQL**, and **MySQL**.
- 🚀 Writing scalable, clean, and maintainable software with **Java (Spring Boot, Quarkus, VRaptor)**, **TypeScript**, and modern ecosystems.
